A. The challenge

With many clients on December year-ends, a typical firm faces a hard annual crunch. Seniors can spend roughly 12 hours per client on bank reconciliation and trial-balance clean-up, then another ~9 hours drafting the SFRS statement set. Peak season means overtime, slipping turnaround, and attrition risk among trained staff.

B. What we built

A four-agent pipeline running on the firm's own files, with a mandatory human checkpoint before any output is released:

  • Ingestion agent — parses bank statements (PDF / CSV) and invoices into structured records.
  • Reconciliation agent — matches transactions and flags only unmatched items for a person to clear.
  • Coding agent — proposes GL codes against the firm's chart of accounts, learning its conventions.
  • FS drafting agent — produces an SFRS for Small Entities statement set with notes and draft XBRL tags.
C. Controls

Client records were processed in an isolated environment with no model training on their data. Every figure traces back to its source document through a full audit log, and a senior signs off before anything reaches a client or ACRA.
